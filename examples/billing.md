# Acorn Workspace Billing

This is fictional billing content for repeatable evaluation, not a real service policy.

Standard annual subscriptions have a 30-day refund request window, measured from the original purchase date. Send a refund request to billing@acorn.example and include the purchase receipt.

This document does not specify how long banks take to post an approved refund, or what happens to refund requests after the standard request window expires.

Billing records and workspace content are separate. Export instructions are in the Acorn Workspace Support document.
