# Acorn Workspace Support

This is fictional support content for repeatable evaluation, not a real service policy.

The Acorn Workspace trial lasts 14 days. No credit card is required to start the trial.

Support is available Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00 in the America/Vancouver time zone.

To export your workspace, open Settings, choose Export, and select JSON or Markdown. Exported files are downloaded to your computer.

These instructions describe the standard service. Enterprise contracts have separate terms and are not part of this support collection.
